Our turning will make God turn: ‘Turn ye unto me, saith the Lord, and I will turn unto you’ (Zechariah, chapter 1, verse 3). He who was an enemy will turn to be our friend. If God turns to us, the angels are turned to us. We shall have their tutelage and guardianship (Psalm 91, verse 11). If God turns to us, all things shall turn to our good, both mercies and afflictions; we shall taste honey at the end of the rod. Thus we have seen the several ingredients of repentance.
